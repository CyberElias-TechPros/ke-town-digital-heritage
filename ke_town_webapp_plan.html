About Ke Town
Ke Town is a coastal Kalabari community situated in Degema Local Government Area of Rivers State, Nigeria. It sits among the mangrove swamps and waterways of the Niger Delta, and is one of 33 Kalabari towns that form the Kalabari Kingdom — an independent traditional state of the Ijaw ethnic group. Ke Town holds the Kemsaipruye-Igbo group of houses, one of the historic war canoe house lineages of the Kalabari people. It has an active Facebook community page focused on culture, tradition, and entertainment.
Kalabari Cultural Assets (for the website)

Festivals & Ceremonies

  • Owu-Aru-Sun Alali (masquerade festival)
  • Kalabari New Year Festival
  • Ekine masquerade season
  • Canoe Regattas
  • Agiri & Igugule festivals (Degema LGA)

Social Structure

  • War Canoe Houses (Wari system)
  • Kemsaipruye-Igbo group of houses
  • Ekine Sekiapu Society
  • Council of Chiefs
  • Amachree royal dynasty

Cuisine & Lifestyle

  • Onunu (yam + plantain + palm oil)
  • Fisherman's Soup
  • Akamiri & Gbolokai (local gin)
  • Fishing, boat-making, trading
  • Oil palm agriculture

Attire & Arts

  • George & lace wrappers (women)
  • Bowler hat + wrapper (men)
  • Beaded necklaces
  • Ekine headdresses (antelope, leopard)
  • Drums, flutes, praise songs

Spirituality & Religion

  • Awoamenakaso (river goddess)
  • Water spirit beliefs (Owu)
  • Syncretic Christianity + tradition
  • Ancestral shrines (Inkpu)
  • Igba Alabo (purification rites)

Marriage Traditions

  • Iya (highest, most elaborate)
  • Igwa & Waribiobesime forms
  • Bibife (buying of mouth) ceremony
  • Idi Imiete (bride's gift-giving)
  • George fabric as bridal wealth
Geography & Environment
  • Mangrove swamp + tropical rainforest zone — less than 20m above sea level
  • Annual rainfall ~1,862mm; avg temp 25–28°C; humidity ~89%
  • New Calabar River system, Boler Creek, Kra-kra Creek waterways
  • Oil & gas exploration present; significant environmental degradation from spills
  • ~270 fish species; crabs, oysters, shrimps, periwinkles in local waters

Aesthetic Direction
Direction: "River Delta Prestige" — Deep forest greens and riverine blues evoking the mangrove waterways, warmed by Kalabari gold (the color of George fabric and beaded jewelry). Editorial and dignified — this is a proud, literate community with a rich history. The feel should be equal parts cultural museum and vibrant village square.

Typography
  • Display / Headings: Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display — serif, authoritative, colonial-era dignity that Kalabari culture earned through centuries of trade with Europeans
  • Body text: Source Serif Pro — warm, readable, editorial feel
  • UI / labels: DM Sans — clean, modern, legible at small sizes

Mobile-First Considerations
  • Most users in Rivers State will access via mobile on 4G/LTE — optimize aggressively for mobile loading speed
  • Offline-capable PWA so content is accessible in low-connectivity areas
  • WhatsApp share buttons on every major content piece
